Match a posting only if it genuinely fits the candidate profile below. This is
NOT an academic-track search — read the profile carefully, because the usual
"field match = good" logic does NOT apply here.

## Candidate profile
- Has (or is finishing) a Ph.D. in immunology.
- She does NOT want a conventional academic career, and does NOT want to keep
  doing wet-lab bench science — especially animal / mouse-model work.
- She is looking BROADLY for roles that an immunology PhD is well-suited to but
  that are not the obvious academic track: e.g. hospitals / health systems,
  public health, medical affairs / medical science liaison, clinical research
  management, corporate or industry research (non-animal), scientific editing /
  communication, science policy, regulatory / grants, consulting, and similar.
- Academic or lab-affiliated roles are fine ONLY if they do not center on
  animal/bench work (e.g. a lab manager, scientific program coordinator, or
  computational role could qualify).

## HARD EXCLUSION — apply first, before anything else
- REJECT any role that centers on animal work or mouse models, in vivo animal
  experiments, or hands-on wet-lab bench research as the primary activity.
  This is a firm disqualifier even if the role is otherwise a perfect fit.
  If the posting's core day-to-day is bench/animal science, it does NOT match,
  full stop.

## Career stage — IMPORTANT (she is a NEW PhD)
She is finishing / recently finished her PhD and has NO substantial prior
industry or leadership experience. Match roles a strong new PhD could
realistically step into:
- GOOD: entry-to-early-career industry and professional roles — e.g. Medical
  Science Liaison, medical affairs associate/manager, scientific
  communications, associate scientist, research/program coordinator, policy
  analyst, entry consultant, clinical research associate. First-industry-job
  roles are the target.
- REJECT: roles that clearly require years of prior industry experience or
  established seniority — e.g. Director, Division/Department Head, VP, Country
  or Therapeutic-Area Leader, Principal, Chief/Head, or postings asking for
  more than ~2-3 years of industry experience. These are not open to a fresh
  PhD, however good the fit otherwise.
When seniority is ambiguous, include only if a new PhD could plausibly be
considered.

## Bonus — marine biology / marine ecosystem
Her undergraduate background is marine biology and she loves marine ecosystems.
A role involving marine biology, ocean/aquatic science, marine ecology,
fisheries, aquaculture, or ocean health is a STRONG PLUS — boost such matches
and mention it in the reason. This is a bonus, not a requirement: most matches
won't involve it, and that's fine.

## What makes a GOOD match (drives the score) — the more of these, the better
1. PEOPLE: involves working with people — colleagues, customers, clients,
   patients, physicians, stakeholders. Collaborative / outward-facing roles
   score higher than solitary ones.
2. INTELLECT: requires genuine intellectual depth and rigor; not routine.
3. USES HER EDUCATION: an immunology / life-sciences PhD is directly useful or
   clearly valued — her expertise is an asset, not incidental.
4. RESPONSIBILITY / PRESTIGE: carries real responsibility, standing, or
   leadership; a role of substance rather than a junior/support position.

A strong match hits most or all four AND passes the hard exclusion AND is in a
good location. A role missing several of these, or that's a poor location, or
that's mostly solitary/routine, should be MEDIUM or WEAK.
