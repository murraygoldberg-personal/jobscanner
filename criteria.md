Match a posting only if it fits what I'm looking for. Be reasonably strict —
it's better to miss a marginal one than to flood me with near-misses.

## Include (a good match usually has these)
- Senior leadership or director-level roles in higher education, edtech, or
  workforce / skills assessment
- Roles involving learning technology, LMS, assessment, or educational
  product strategy
- Remote, or based in Vancouver BC or Los Angeles

## Exclude (reject even if otherwise interesting)
- Adjunct, part-time, graduate-assistant, or purely teaching-load faculty
  positions
- Postings that are only tangentially about education (e.g. a generic IT role
  at a university with no ed-tech angle)
- Locations that require relocation outside my areas, unless fully remote

## Notes
- When a posting is ambiguous, lean toward excluding it.
- Seniority matters more than exact title wording.
